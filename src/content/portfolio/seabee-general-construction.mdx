## Project overview

SeaBee General Contractor is a marine construction website I built for a Florida company serving Marco Island. The company positions itself as a premium marine builder and has a brand partnership with Fiberon, so the site was designed to carry Fiberon branding across key sections and product-focused pages.

The website covers their core services, including boat lifts, marine docks, re-decking, and seawalls, while also presenting product listings for Fiberon decking.

![Seabee Partner section - Fiberon.](/images/portfolio/seabee-1.webp)

## Challenge

The challenge was combining service-led marketing with product catalog content in one consistent experience. The site needed to communicate SeaBee's premium positioning while staying practical for lead generation and day-to-day content updates.

## Solution

I built the project with **WordPress + WooCommerce** for product listings, then used **ACF** to structure custom post types for boat lift entries. The rest of the site is organized into service, gallery, about, and contact sections, with Fiberon branding integrated throughout.

The result is a brand-consistent marine construction website that supports both service inquiries and product discovery.
