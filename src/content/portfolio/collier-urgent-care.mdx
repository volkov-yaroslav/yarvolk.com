## Project overview

Collier Urgent Care was one of my earlier WordPress builds for a healthcare client in Naples, Florida. The center operates across three locations, so the website needed to present services and location-specific information in a clear, patient-friendly format.

The final structure includes service pages, doctor information, location details, operating hours, holiday updates, FAQs, and a dedicated employment application page.

![About Collier Urgent Care.](/images/portfolio/collier-urgent-care-1.webp)

## Challenge

The challenge was to keep medical and operational information easy to scan while maintaining a consistent structure across three clinic locations. The site also needed to support both patient questions and hiring workflows.

## Solution

I built the site with **WordPress**, using **Bridge Theme** and **Elementor** to implement the required page templates and content sections quickly. I also added structured pages for locations and an employment application flow to support staffing needs.

The result is a practical urgent care website that improves access to key information and supports both patient communication and hiring.
