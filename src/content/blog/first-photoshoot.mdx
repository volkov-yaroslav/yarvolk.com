On **August 14, 2026**, I had my first professional photoshoot in **Wrocław**. I wanted a set of photos I could use across my social media, websites, and other projects.

The idea was to look professional while keeping things casual. Something that felt like me, without being too formal.

## One hour in the studio

I hired **Anna**, a local photographer who is also from Ukraine, and rented a studio for one hour. We went for a relaxed look, with casual clothes and a few different settings.

I create websites for businesses, so naturally my **MacBook** had to be part of the photoshoot too. It made sense to include something that is such a big part of my everyday work alongside the portraits.
